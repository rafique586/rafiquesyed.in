Start with a real observation, experience, or question. Keep the opening human and specific.

## A useful section heading

Write in short paragraphs. Explain the idea without making the reader work through unnecessary jargon.

> Use a blockquote for the one sentence you want the reader to remember.

## A practical section

1. Give the reader a concrete first step.
2. Explain what good looks like.
3. End with a question or principle they can carry into their own work.

## Where this leaves us

Close with your honest view. A good field note should leave the reader with something useful, not simply announce that the topic is important.
